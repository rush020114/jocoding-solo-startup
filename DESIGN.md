## Brand & Style

This design system translates the intimacy of a dedicated personal stylist into an editorial, magazine-grade mobile web experience. It bridges contemporary high-fashion publication aesthetics (reminiscent of *Vogue*, *Kinfolk*, and *Gentlewoman*) with tactile, intuitive AI-driven interaction.

### Aesthetic Tone
- **Editorial Poise:** Ample spatial pauses, asymmetrical split-column compositions, and high-contrast typographic titling evoke tactile paper stock and glossy fashion spreads.
- **Warm Tactility:** Moving away from sterile algorithmic interfaces, surfaces leverage warm ecru, sun-baked terracotta, and soft stone tones.
- **Modern Minimalist Luxe:** Clean structural framing, delicate translucent floating overlays, and organic, generous border radii provide a balance between haute couture heritage and cutting-edge digital efficiency.

### Target Audience & Experience
Catering to design-conscious individuals, stylists, and modern shoppers seeking curated outfit recommendations, capsule wardrobe generation, and instantaneous look critiques. The interface should feel calm, luxurious, and deliberate—free from visual clutter and algorithmic noise.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic luxury tone through layered warm neutrals punctuated by earthen accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#C06B3E` - Terracotta):** Represents artisanal craftsmanship, leather accessories, and editorial confidence. Applied to primary call-to-actions, active indicators, and high-priority AI insights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#D97706` - Warm Caramel):** Used sparingly for tertiary highlights, subtle badge fills, ratings, and auxiliary interactive states.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FAF8F5`): A luminous, warm ecru canvas evocative of heavy-grain editorial paper.
  - Surface Subdued (`#F3EFEA`): Soft beige tone for structural cards, inset collections, and contextual drawers.
  - Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF`): Reserved for active floating cards, interactive sheets, and crisp contrast moments.
- **Typography & Dark Contrast:**
  - Primary Ink (`#18181B`): Deep neutral black delivering sharp, legible editorial headlines.
  - Secondary Ink (`#27272A`): Muted charcoal for body text and descriptive subtitles.
  - Tertiary / Muted Ink (`#71717A`): Subdued tone for timestamps, metadata, and fine print.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Low-contrast hairline dividers (`#E7E2DA`) maintain structural discipline without visual heaviness.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy blends high-contrast Italian Didone serifs with ultra-legible, contemporary Swiss-inspired grotesk sans-serifs.

### Role Mapping
- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers distinct couture sophistication. Used strictly for lookbook titles, editorial volume markers, category introductions, and mood labels. Headlines italicize key fashion verbs and stylistic prompts for editorial flair.
- **Body & Captions (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Provides sharp, clean functional reading across styling notes, fit critiques, and AI diagnostic feedback.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (`Hanken Grotesk`, All-Caps):** Used for navigation, collection tags, seasonal badges, and metadata. Increased letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) enhances visual authority and chic minimalism.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout configured to evoke the rhythmic white space of high-end lookbooks.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column and 2-column staggered asymmetrical feeds with `1.25rem` screen margins and `1rem` gutters. Horizontally scrolling lookbook carousels allow elements to peek into viewport edges.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column grid with `2rem` screen margins, enabling split layouts featuring an AI outfit preview panel alongside the curated item drawer.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column fixed grid with an upscale max-width of `1280px` centered within a neutral canvas. Employs magazine spreads where editorial hero imagery commands 7 columns and styling diagnostics span 5 columns.

### Spacing Principles
- Generous outer margins isolate items, allowing textures and silhouettes breathing room.
- Micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) binds closely tied metadata (brand tag + item title).
- Macro-spacing (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) clearly distinguishes lookbook chapters, seasons, and AI recommendation modules.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, instead employing ambient, warm diffuse illumination and tonal layering that mimics fine art photography prints.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground (`#FAF8F5`):** Base canvas layer.
- **Surface Inset (`#F3EFEA`):** Recessed containers, wardrobe slot placeholders, and image gallery backgrounds.
- **Floating Sheet (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level panels, modal overlays, and sticky bottom sheets.

### Shadow Character
Shadows are ultra-diffuse and tinted with deep warm umber rather than stark grays:
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(39, 39, 42, 0.04)`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Look):** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(192, 107, 62, 0.08), 0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04)`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Wardrobe Drawers):** `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.10)`

### Editorial Glassmorphism
Floating action controls, bottom navigation bars, and top editorial headers utilize a soft translucent glass blur:
- `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
- `background: rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)`
- `border: 1px solid rgba(231, 226, 218, 0.6)`

## Shapes

The shape system balances architectural discipline with soft, organic curves. Primary cards, imagery masks, and floating drawers utilize refined curvatures (`1rem` to `1.5rem`), referencing modern furniture design and sculpted fabric drapery.

### Geometry Scale
- **Containers & Big Cards:** `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) and `rounded-3xl` (1.5rem / 24px) are the standard for fashion look previews, outfit recommendation containers, and wardrobe cards.
- **Buttons & Interactive Chips:** Strict pill styling (`rounded-full`) creates an intentional, tactile contrast with structured rectangular fashion photography.
- **Input Fields & Small Surfaces:** `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) balances precision and approachability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Terracotta (`#C06B3E`) with crisp white typography in uppercase (`label-lg`). Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), minimum height of `48px` for optimal mobile touch target. Active state deepens to `#A8582E`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Outlined with a fine `1px` border in `#18181B` or `#E7E2DA` over a transparent or white ground. High-contrast hover and press feedback.
- **AI Action ("Curate" / "Generate"):** Terracotta background with subtle caramel gradient shimmer border and high-gloss soft umber shadow.

### Cards
- **Outfit Preview Card:** Clean elevated white surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed by an ultra-fine border (`#E7E2DA`). Image features an edge-to-edge bleed or `space-sm` matte inset border. Subtle `rounded-2xl` radius.
- **Styling Insight Card:** `#F3EFEA` background with zero border. Uses `Bodoni Moda` italic drop caps or terracotta iconography for AI advice commentary.

### Chips & Filters
- **Aesthetic Tags (e.g., "Quiet Luxury", "Avant-Garde", "Monochrome"):** Pill-shaped, `32px` height. Default state features a neutral `#F3EFEA` fill with `#27272A` text. Selected state inverts to `#18181B` fill with `#FAF8F5` text, or `#C06B3E` fill for active AI filters.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Search & AI Prompt Bar:** Pill-shaped or `rounded-2xl` input shell with `#FFFFFF` fill and a recessed, soft shadow. Placeholder text set in italicized `Hanken Grotesk` (`#71717A`). Focus ring transitions smoothly to a `1px` Terracotta outline with zero harsh haloing.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Selection Controls:** Custom circular check triggers (`rounded-full`). Selected state exhibits a solid Terracotta ring with a centered terracotta pill or micro-check icon.

### Specialized Editorial Components
- **Wardrobe Slot Indicator:** Segmented pill bar with low-contrast fill indicating outfit harmony, color palette balance, and seasonal appropriateness.
- **Floating Wardrobe Bottom Bar:** Docked pill container centered with `margin` offsets, delivering swift thumb-level access to the camera scanner, virtual closet, and daily style prompt.
